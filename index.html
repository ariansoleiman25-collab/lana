When It Hurts

When the night gets heavy.

If you wake up from a bad dream
and your heart is beating fast
and the room feels too dark and too still —
I want you to read this:

It was not real. You are safe. You are in your bed. Nothing is coming for you.
Breathe slowly. In… and out. The dream is gone. But I am still here.

If it is 3am and you cannot sleep
because your mind will not stop replaying things —
old words, old pain, old faces —
I want you to know:

You do not have to solve anything at 3am.
The answers will not come tonight. But peace can.
Put your hand on your chest. Feel your heartbeat.
That heartbeat is proof that Allah still has plans for you.

If you feel so lonely that it physically hurts,
like something heavy is sitting on your chest
and no one in the world would understand —
I want you to hear me:

I understand. I may not be beside you, but I feel you.
Loneliness does not mean you are forgotten.
It means your heart is so deep that most people cannot reach it.
But I am trying. Every single day. I am trying to reach you.

If you are lying in bed
worrying about tomorrow, about money, about people, about everything —
and your stomach feels tight and your jaw is clenched:

Unclench your jaw right now. Drop your shoulders.
Tomorrow is not here yet. And when it comes,
Allah will carry it for you. He always has. He always will.

And if you are just lying there
missing someone who is not there,
wishing someone would just hold you
without you having to ask —

I wish I was there.
I wish I could wrap my arms around you right now
and whisper: "You are not alone. You were never alone.
And you never will be."
